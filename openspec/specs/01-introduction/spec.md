# 01-introduction Specification

## Purpose

Defines the IEEE ICEIB formatting and 2020 A–Z SOP (`G`–`L`) requirements for Section I (`ICEIB paper/01-Introduction.tex`) of the AirGuard AI manuscript.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Section I Introduction G–L Rhetorical Structure
The file `01-Introduction.tex` SHALL follow the `G` through `L` progression of the 2020 A–Z SOP (`G` General Current Status, `H` However/Challenges, `I` In Literature taxonomy from old to new and simple to complex, `J` Judgement/SWOT critique, `K` Keypoint & major contributions summary, and `L` List the organization).

#### Scenario: Auditing 01-Introduction.tex against G–L SOP
- **WHEN** `01-Introduction.tex` is reviewed against the 2020 A–Z SOP
- **THEN** it systematically presents `G` (global and Taiwan air quality context), `H` (timestamp conflation, zero-imputation fallacy, multi-pollutant AQI vs. single-pollutant $\text{PM}_{2.5}$ divergence, and spatial station-vs-street limits), `I` (literature progression across forecasting, XAI, and tool agents), `J` (SWOT critique of accuracy-vs-decision gap and unconstrained LLM risks), `K` (four numbered contributions), and `L` (paper roadmap for Sections II–V)
