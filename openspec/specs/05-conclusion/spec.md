# 05-conclusion Specification

## Purpose

Defines the IEEE ICEIB formatting and 2020 A–Z SOP (`W`–`Z`) requirements for Section V and the Acknowledgment (`ICEIB paper/05-Conclusion.tex`).

## Requirements

### Requirement: Section V Conclusion W–Z Structure
The file `05-Conclusion.tex` SHALL follow the `W` through `Z` progression of the 2020 A–Z SOP (`W` What Proposed recap, `X` eXcel scenario and methodological strengths, `Y` Yields from Phase W1 empirical audit and dual-track framework, and `Z` Zen scope limitations and Phases W2–W6 future work).

- **WHEN** `05-Conclusion.tex` is reviewed
- **THEN** it systematically covers `W` (motivation and AirGuard AI recap), `X` (station-scale dual-track and constrained ReAct advantages), `Y` (123,055 verified Taichung hourly observations, 95.49%–97.90% coverage, and cryptographic SHA256 reproducibility), and `Z` (exclusion of street-level routing/inhaled dose/medical diagnosis and roadmap for W2–W6 backtesting, P1 SHAP, and web deployment)
